Proprietary methodology

TPA — Think, Plan and Action

A proprietary methodology designed to turn communication into strategy.

THINK

Think before executing

Before any content is created, the agency enters a deep stage of analysis and study to understand the business, the audience and the desired positioning.

  • Persona research
  • Competitive analysis
  • Brand pillars
  • SWOT analysis
  • Current business moment
  • Content DNA
  • Editorial lines
  • Tone of voice
  • Brand personality
PLAN

Building the strategic path

We translate insights into a clear communication system by defining what the brand should say, how it should show up, which narratives it should own and how campaigns connect to business goals.

  • Content strategies
  • Campaigns
  • Timelines
  • Creative directions
  • Communication formats
  • Growth strategies
  • Execution planning
ACTION

Strategy that stays involved.

Strategy only matters when it survives the real world. That’s why we remain involved in content, campaigns, performance and ongoing decisions. We learn from the market and refine the direction continuously.

  • Content production
  • Publishing
  • Campaigns
  • Social media management
  • Results tracking
  • Monthly meetings
  • Strategic improvements
  • Seasonal actions

About the agency

A boutique agency built on strategy, creativity and close collaboration.

The Power Agency combines strategic thinking, creative direction and execution to build brands with clarity, relevance and intention.

We work closely with a select number of clients, creating tailored strategies rather than replicating formulas.

Every project is shaped around the business, the audience and the moment of the brand.

Meet the founders

Two minds. One direction.

The Power Agency is led by Rafaela and Ana, two different perspectives brought together by more than a decade of friendship, trust and shared ambition.

Rafaela brings the strategic mind and Ana brings the creative lens.

That combination shapes the way we work at The Power Agency, because strategy and creativity are never treated as separate disciplines. Every idea needs direction. Every strategy needs a way to come alive.

And because we have worked, thought and built alongside each other for years, our clients don’t get disconnected departments or layers of communication.

They get two complementary minds thinking closely about the same brand. Different strengths, the same standard.

Not for everyone

The right fit matters.

We do our best work with brands that see marketing as part of the business and not just as a content calendar.

The Power Agency is built for companies that value strategy, positioning and creative direction as much as visibility.

We work best with brands that:

  • want strategic involvement, not just execution;
  • are open to direction, refinement and new perspectives;
  • value consistency over isolated actions;
  • understand that strong communication starts with clear positioning;
  • want a close, ongoing relationship with the people thinking about their brand.

If you’re looking for a partner who can think with you, challenge ideas when needed and turn strategy into consistent action, we may be the right fit.
